# Element Forge - Complete Crafting Table

## 📚 Crafting System Overview

The crafting system allows players to forge new cards during gameplay using **Elemental Essences**. This document contains all craftable cards with their recipes, stats, and abilities.

## 🧪 Essence Types

| Essence | Element | How to Obtain |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| 🔥 Fire Essence | Fire | Drop from Fire creatures, random from hand |
| 💧 Water Essence | Water | Drop from Water creatures, random from hand |
| 🌍 Earth Essence | Earth | Drop from Earth creatures, random from hand |
| 💨 Air Essence | Air | Drop from Air creatures, random from hand |
| 🌑 Void Essence | Void | Drop from Void creatures, random from hand |

## 🔄 Crafting Mechanics Summary

### During Gameplay
1. **Collect Essences**: Gained during Energy Phase and when creatures die
2. **Open Crafting Menu**: During your Main Phase
3. **Select Recipe**: Choose from available recipes you have essences for
4. **Craft Card**: Essences consumed, card added to hand
5. **Limit**: Maximum 3 crafts per turn

### Strategic Tips
- **Sacrifice Strategy**: Sometimes destroying your own weak creatures for essences is worth it
- **Essence Banking**: Save essences for powerful late-game crafts
- **Emergency Crafting**: Craft defensive creatures when under pressure
- **Combo Setup**: Craft specific cards to enable powerful combinations
- **Element Focus**: Build your deck around 2-3 elements for consistent crafting
