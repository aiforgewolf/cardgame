# Element Forge - Official Rulebook

## 🎮 Game Overview

**Element Forge** is a strategic 2-player online card game where players take on the role of Elemental Mages, summoning creatures and casting spells to defeat their opponent. The unique crafting system allows you to forge new cards during gameplay using elemental essences.

## 🎯 Objective

Win by achieving one of two victory conditions:
1. **Destruction Victory**: Reduce your opponent's Life Points to 0
2. **Nexus Control Victory**: Control the Nexus (center battlefield) for 3 consecutive turns

## 🃏 Card Types

### 1. Creature Cards
- Can be summoned to the battlefield
- Have Attack, Defense, and Energy cost
- Can attack opponents or defend your Nexus
- Have special abilities based on their element

### 2. Spell Cards
- Instant or ongoing effects
- Consumed when played (unless specified)
- Can buff allies, damage enemies, or manipulate the board

### 3. Artifact Cards
- Permanent objects that provide ongoing benefits
- Remain on the battlefield until destroyed
- Can generate resources or modify game rules

## ⚡ The Five Elements

Each card belongs to one of five elements, each with unique characteristics:

### 🔥 Fire
- **Strength**: High attack damage
- **Weakness**: Lower defense
- **Special Trait**: Burn (deals damage over multiple turns)
- **Essence Color**: Red

### 💧 Water
- **Strength**: Healing and defense
- **Weakness**: Lower attack
- **Special Trait**: Flow (can redirect damage)
- **Essence Color**: Blue

### 🌍 Earth
- **Strength**: High defense and durability
- **Weakness**: Higher energy costs
- **Special Trait**: Fortify (gains defense each turn)
- **Essence Color**: Green

### 💨 Air
- **Strength**: Speed and evasion
- **Weakness**: Lower health
- **Special Trait**: Swift (can attack immediately when summoned)
- **Essence Color**: Yellow

### 🌑 Void
- **Strength**: Disruption and card manipulation
- **Weakness**: Unpredictable effects
- **Special Trait**: Banish (removes cards from play permanently)
- **Essence Color**: Purple

## 📊 Game Setup

### Starting Conditions
- **Life Points**: Each player starts with 30 Life Points
- **Starting Hand**: Draw 5 cards
- **Starting Deck**: Minimum 20 cards, maximum 40 cards
- **Energy**: Start with 1 Energy, gain +1 max Energy per turn (up to 10)
- **Essence Pool**: Start with 0 essences

### Deck Building Rules
- Minimum 20 cards, maximum 40 cards
- Maximum 3 copies of any single card (except Basic cards)
- Must contain at least 12 Creature cards
- Can mix elements freely

## 🎲 Turn Structure

### Phase 1: Draw Phase
1. Draw 2 cards from your deck
2. If you can't draw, you take 2 damage (Fatigue)

### Phase 2: Energy Phase
1. Gain +1 maximum Energy (max 10)
2. Restore all Energy to maximum
3. Gain 1 random Essence based on cards in your hand

### Phase 3: Main Phase
You may perform any of these actions in any order:
- **Summon Creatures**: Pay Energy cost to place creatures on battlefield
- **Cast Spells**: Pay Energy cost to activate spell effects
- **Play Artifacts**: Pay Energy cost to place artifacts
- **Craft Cards**: Use essences to forge new cards (see Crafting System)
- **Activate Abilities**: Use creature or artifact abilities

### Phase 4: Combat Phase
1. Declare attackers from your creatures
2. Opponent declares blockers
3. Resolve combat damage
4. Check for Nexus control

### Phase 5: End Phase
1. Discard down to 7 cards if over limit
2. Resolve end-of-turn effects
3. Check for victory conditions

## ⚔️ Combat System

### Attacking
- Each creature can attack once per turn (unless it has Swift or just entered)
- Creatures cannot attack on the turn they're summoned (except Air element with Swift)
- Choose to attack the opponent directly OR attack the Nexus

### Defending & Blocking
- Defending player can assign creatures to block attackers
- One blocker per attacker
- Blocked creatures deal damage to each other
- Unblocked creatures deal damage to their target

### Damage Resolution
- Attack vs Defense: Attacker deals damage equal to Attack, defender deals damage equal to Defense
- If a creature takes damage ≥ its Defense, it's destroyed
- Excess damage doesn't carry over (unless card effect specifies)

### Nexus Control
- The Nexus is the center battlefield zone
- Control the Nexus by having more total creature Attack power in the Nexus zone than your opponent
- If you control the Nexus for 3 consecutive end phases, you win!

## 🔨 Crafting System

### Essence Collection
- Gain 1 random Essence during Energy Phase based on element distribution in hand
- Gain Essences when creatures are destroyed (they drop their element essence)
- Some cards grant bonus Essences

### Crafting Rules
- Can craft during your Main Phase
- Crafted cards go directly to your hand
- Essences are consumed when crafting
- Can only craft cards from the Crafting Table (not custom)
- Maximum 3 crafts per turn

## 💰 Card Shop & Progression

### Currency System
- **Gold**: Earned by playing matches (10 per loss, 25 per win)
- **Crystals**: Premium currency (can be earned or purchased)

### Purchasing Cards
- **Starter Packs**: 5 random cards - 50 Gold
- **Elemental Packs**: 5 cards of chosen element - 100 Gold
- **Premium Pack**: 5 cards with guaranteed rare+ - 200 Gold or 2 Crystals
- **Individual Cards**: Purchase specific cards
  - Common: 20 Gold
  - Uncommon: 50 Gold
  - Rare: 100 Gold
  - Epic: 250 Gold
  - Legendary: 500 Gold

### Guest vs Registered Players
- **Guest Players**:
  - Can play unlimited matches
  - Start with a basic deck
  - Progress NOT saved between sessions
  - Cannot purchase cards
  - Cannot access crafting during deck building (only in-game)

- **Registered Players**:
  - All progress saved
  - Build and save multiple decks
  - Purchase card packs and individual cards
  - Access to ranked matchmaking
  - Earn daily login rewards
  - Participate in seasonal events

## 🎯 Strategy Tips

1. **Energy Curve**: Balance low and high-cost cards for consistent plays
2. **Essence Planning**: Destroy your own creatures strategically to gain essences for crafting
3. **Nexus Pressure**: Force opponent to choose between attacking and defending Nexus
4. **Element Synergy**: Build decks around 2-3 elements for reliable crafting
5. **Tempo vs Value**: Decide whether to play aggressively or build resources

## 🏆 Victory Conditions Summary

1. **Destruction**: Reduce opponent to 0 Life Points
2. **Nexus Control**: Control the Nexus for 3 consecutive end phases
3. **Deck Out**: Opponent cannot draw cards (takes fatigue damage)
4. **Special Win**: Certain cards like "Elemental Convergence" can win instantly

## 📜 Additional Rules

### Card Rarity
- **Basic**: Available to all players, unlimited copies
- **Common**: Easy to obtain, 3 copies max
- **Uncommon**: Moderate effects, 3 copies max
- **Rare**: Strong effects, 3 copies max
- **Epic**: Very powerful, 3 copies max
- **Legendary**: Game-changing effects, 3 copies max

### Special Abilities Glossary
- **Swift**: Can attack the turn it's summoned
- **Taunt**: Must be blocked if able
- **Fortify X**: Gains +X Defense at end of turn
- **Burn X**: Deals X damage at start of opponent's turn
- **Flow**: Can redirect damage to another target
- **Banish**: Remove from game permanently (not just destroyed)
- **Resurrect**: Returns to battlefield when destroyed
- **Ward**: Immune to next spell or ability

### Timing and Priority
- Active player has priority during their turn
- Opponent can respond to actions with "Instant" spells
- Stack resolves in reverse order (last in, first out)
